# Limitations and responsible use

- **Coverage:** the live register is not the whole UK business population. The accounts-category proxy omits many SMEs and cannot verify employees, turnover, independence or group structure.
- **Survivorship and selection:** dissolved companies are outside the intended live-register frame. An eligible historical accounts category selects established filers. Neither this sample nor cohort comparisons can estimate SME failure rates.
- **Snapshot, not panel:** one observed release cannot show previous status, score changes, director velocity, causal ordering or validated early-warning lead time. Do not confuse month-end reference with real-time availability.
- **Public filings:** company-supplied information may be inaccurate, delayed, amended or incomplete. A missed deadline can reflect administrative problems, extensions or registry update timing; verify the current official record manually.
- **Missing finances:** no profitability, cash flow, debt-service coverage, employee counts, balance sheets or financial ratios were acquired. Outstanding charges can reflect ordinary borrowing rather than deterioration.
- **Status ambiguity:** strike-off proposals need not be insolvency; liquidation is not subtyped; dissolution includes legitimate closure. Do not automatically use any of them as a universal financial-distress label.
- **False positives:** a high filing review score can flag a healthy company. **False negatives:** a financially distressed business may file on time and score zero. The index does not estimate either error rate.
- **Policy sensitivity:** weights, caps and bands are judgemental. The sensitivity tables quantify queue changes but cannot validate economic meaning. No probability calibration or predictive performance exists.
- **Geography:** registered-office postcode areas may identify agents or accountants and can cross administrative boundaries. They do not represent operating regions, employee locations or geographic density.
- **Industry:** first listed SIC is not guaranteed to be the main economic activity. Multi-activity classification and industry-specific filing patterns can affect apparent concentrations.
- **Uncertainty:** sample-based intervals ignore filing errors, nonresponse, proxy misclassification and multiple-ranking selection. Age-standardised comparisons do not remove all confounding.
- **Temporal and historical bias:** prospective snapshots may reflect policy changes, different accounting thresholds, registry enforcement or economic cycles. Any future predictive model would need drift monitoring and recalibration; this release is an uncalibrated index.
- **Privacy:** raw downloads are excluded from Git. The sample discards street addresses, full postcodes, officer names and dates of birth. Optional API outputs retain only minimal event metadata. Publicly accessible does not remove obligations concerning personal data.
- **Licence:** code is MIT; source data is not relicensed by the code licence. The official bulk page describes free access but does not itself clearly state a data-specific redistribution licence. Publish aggregate outputs and download instructions; review applicable source terms before redistributing row-level source data.

Intended for analytical/educational decision support and manual registry review. This tool must not independently determine lending, investment, employment or other high-impact decisions. It is not financial advice and should not be used as an automated credit-decision system.
